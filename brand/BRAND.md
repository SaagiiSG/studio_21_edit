Slide themes: light (Graphite heading, Slate body, Violet accent) · dark (Cloud heading, Mist body, Mint
accent) · violet (White heading, Lilac body, White accent) · lilac (Graphite heading, Slate body, Violet accent).

## Type

- **Space Grotesk** Bold / Medium: headlines, numbers. IG sizes: Display 128 px (LH 100 %, LS −3 %),
  Headline 92 px, Title 64 px, Subhead 44 px Medium.
- **Inter** Regular / Medium / Semi Bold: body, labels. IG Label: Inter Semi Bold 26 px, caps, LS 8 %.
- No other fonts. Both are free Google Fonts (OFL).

## Do

- One accent per slide: Signal Violet OR Mint, not both on text.
- Mint is for dots, highlights and text on Graphite only.
- Keep text inside the 96 px margins (IG crops the grid to 3:4).
- Label every example as EXAMPLE. No invented results or stats.
- Plain words: "replies", "reminders", "follow-ups".

## Don't

- No brown, tan, gold, or navy/brass.
- No gendered colour coding.
- No robots, glowing brains, circuit boards or green-on-black code.
- No fonts outside Space Grotesk + Inter.
- No text in Mint on Cloud/White, or Mint on Violet.
- No fake testimonials, logos, or client counts.
